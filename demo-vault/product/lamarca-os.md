# LAMARCA OS

LAMARCA OS is the spatial computing tab inside [[synapse]]. A full screen 3D interface you drive with voice and your hands, no mouse needed.

## What it does
- A floating carousel of living modules: Weather, News, Stocks, Instagram, Calendar, Music, Sports, System, Projects, AI.
- Weather and News pull real live data with no API key. The rest show rich charts and stats.
- Ask anything in the command bar or with your voice, it answers and talks back.
- Hand tracking runs in the browser: open palm wakes it, swipe rotates the carousel, pinch selects, fist goes home, two hands zoom. A blue mesh draws over your hand on the camera.
- The room reacts to the module you open: rain for Weather, warm light for Projects, deep space for System.
- One tap switches back to the [[synapse]] brain. The two are complementary.

## Why it matters
It shows where a personal AI operating system is going: you stop clicking and start pointing, speaking, and moving. It is the demo that makes people feel the potential of a [[second-brain]] you can walk into.

Built on plain web tech, offline first, no install. Runs on phone and desktop.
