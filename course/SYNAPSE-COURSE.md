## MODULE 1 — START HERE

### Lesson 1.1 — Your notes are dead. Let us fix that.

**A folder of notes is a graveyard. A brain is alive.**

You have hundreds of notes. You never reread them. They sit in folders you forgot.
The problem is not you. It is the shape. A list hides how ideas connect. Your real
thinking is a web, not a column.

SYNAPSE reads your notes and draws that web. Every note becomes a node. Every link
becomes a synapse. Suddenly you see the shape of what you know, and the holes in it.

What you get in this course:
- See your notes as a living 2D and 3D brain
- Ask JARVIS questions and get answers from your own notes
- Capture, organise and grow your brain without lifting a finger
- White label it and sell it as your own product

**BUILD:** Open your notes folder right now. Count the notes you have not opened in a
month. That number is the point of this course.

**PROOF:** "I had 400 notes and used maybe 10. SYNAPSE showed me the other 390 were
all connected. I just could not see it."

### Lesson 1.2 — Install SYNAPSE in two minutes

**No Python. No setup. Double click and your brain opens.**

Go to the release page and grab your file:
- Windows: SYNAPSE.exe
- Mac: SYNAPSE-mac.zip, then unzip
- Link: https://github.com/bruceleeFR/synapse/releases/tag/v1.0.0

Windows: double click SYNAPSE.exe. If SmartScreen warns, click More info, then Run
anyway. The app is safe, it is just not code signed yet.

Mac: unzip, then right click SYNAPSE.app and choose Open. Right click matters on the
first launch, a normal double click gets blocked because the app is unsigned.

Your browser opens on a demo brain so you can look around before you point it at your
own notes.

**BUILD:** Install it and let the demo brain load. Drag to move, scroll to zoom. Click
a node and read it.

**PROOF:** "Zero install pain. I clicked the file and I was flying through a 3D galaxy
of notes ten seconds later."

---

### Lesson 1.3 — Point it at your own notes

**Any folder of markdown works. Obsidian works as is.**

SYNAPSE reads plain markdown. If you use Obsidian, your vault already is a folder of
markdown with wikilinks, so it just works. If you use plain notes, put them in one
folder.

Run the app with a folder path to read your notes instead of the demo:
- Windows: SYNAPSE.exe "C:\Users\me\Notes"
- Mac: right click Open once, then from a terminal ./SYNAPSE.app/Contents/MacOS/SYNAPSE ~/Notes

Two things power the graph:
- Wikilinks: writing [[Another Note]] draws a link between the two notes
- Folders and tags: they colour and group your brain

**BUILD:** Point SYNAPSE at your real notes. Watch your own brain appear. Find your
biggest hub, the note everything connects to.

**PROOF:** "I pointed it at my Obsidian vault and my whole business was just there, as a
map. My client work, my offers, my content, all in one glowing picture."

## MODULE 2 — EXPLORE YOUR BRAIN

### Lesson 2.1 — Read the 2D graph

**Big glowing balls are your important notes. Lines are your ideas connecting.**

The 2D view is your brain from above. A node is a note. Its size grows with how many
links it has and how much you open it. The bright, big ones are your hubs, the ideas
everything hangs off.

- Hover a node to light up its neighbours
- Click a node to read it, with its links out and its backlinks
- Drag a node to pull it around, the brain settles back like elastic

**BUILD:** Find your three biggest hubs. Those are the pillars of how you think. Ask
yourself if that is the story you want your brain to tell.

**PROOF:** "My biggest hub was a note I wrote two years ago and forgot. Everything I do
still traces back to it."

---

### Lesson 2.2 — Fly the 3D brain

**The same brain, in three dimensions, with cinema grade glow.**

Hit 3D in the rail. Your notes become a galaxy. Same nodes, same links, now in space,
with real bloom and synapses firing between the balls. Related notes cluster into clear
lobes by theme, so the shape of your knowledge is obvious at a glance.

- Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom
- Every ball shows its name, so you read the brain without clicking
- Click a ball to open the full note

**BUILD:** Orbit your brain in 3D. Notice the lobes. Each cluster is a part of your life
or business. Name them out loud.

**PROOF:** "The 3D view made me realise my content and my offers barely connect. That
gap was costing me sales."

### Lesson 2.3 — Search, filter, follow

**Find any note in a second. Follow a thought across your whole brain.**

- The search bar filters your brain live as you type
- Folder chips let you hide or show whole areas
- In a note panel, the links out and backlinks are clickable, so you can walk your
  ideas from note to note
- Press Cmd or Ctrl and K for the command palette, then type to jump to any note or run
  any action

**BUILD:** Open the palette with Cmd or Ctrl and K. Type a word from an old note and
jump straight to it. Then follow its backlinks and see where that idea traveled.

**PROOF:** "The command palette turned my notes into something I actually navigate,
like a codebase, not a pile."

## MODULE 3 — MEET JARVIS

### Lesson 3.1 — Turn JARVIS on

**One key, stored on your machine, and your brain starts talking back.**

The graph and every effect work with no key. To let JARVIS answer, open Tune JARVIS in
the rail and paste an OpenAI key. Want Grok, Gemini or others, add an OpenRouter key
too.

Your key is saved locally in your vault config, and it never leaves your machine. Only
the notes relevant to your question are sent to the model, and only when you ask.

**BUILD:** Paste your key in Tune JARVIS. Watch the status turn to Brain connected.

**PROOF:** "I was scared about privacy. Then I saw the key sits in my own folder and
only my relevant notes get sent. That sold me."

---

### Lesson 3.2 — Ask your own brain anything

**Not the internet. Your notes. Answered in your words.**

Open JARVIS and ask a real question about your work. It pulls the notes that matter,
answers from them, flies the graph to the exact source, lights it up, and reads it back
to you. Every answer shows the source notes so you can trust it.

Try questions like:
- What offers do I sell and who buys them
- What did I decide about pricing last month
- Which client is on a retainer

**BUILD:** Ask JARVIS one question you would normally dig through folders to answer.
Watch it fly to the source.

**PROOF:** "I asked what my three offers were. It answered in five seconds and flew to
each one. My own notes, talking to me."

---

### Lesson 3.3 — Call JARVIS, live

**Press the phone. Talk. It listens, answers out loud, and listens again.**

Hit Call JARVIS. A full screen call opens with a glowing orb that reacts to your real
voice. You talk, it thinks, it answers out loud, and it goes back to listening on its
own. It is a hands free conversation with your own brain.

You can drive the whole app by voice too: say go 3D, fit the view, brain gaps, brief
me, or switch to a different model.

**BUILD:** Start a call and ask something while your hands are busy. End the call when
you are done.

**PROOF:** "I do the dishes and interview my own brain. That sentence sounds insane and
it is completely real."

---

### Lesson 3.4 — Brain Gaps, the links you are missing

**JARVIS finds the connections your notes should have and did not.**

Hit Brain Gaps. SYNAPSE finds notes that clearly belong together but were never linked,
draws them as dashed synapses, and tells you why. Each suggestion has a plus link
button. One tap writes the link into your notes for real. Your brain tidies itself.

**BUILD:** Run Brain Gaps. Apply the strongest suggestion. Watch a new synapse become
permanent.

**PROOF:** "It found that two client notes and my retainer offer all talked about the
same thing but never linked. One tap and my brain got smarter."

### Lesson 5.2 — Package it as an offer

**A second brain is not a feature. It is an outcome people pay for.**

Ways to sell it:
- Done for you: you set up a client's second brain from their docs and hand it over
- Productised service: a monthly retainer keeping their brain tidy and answering
- Bundle: pair it with your existing coaching or consulting as the memory layer
- Lead magnet: a branded demo brain that shows a prospect their own mess, then your fix

Price the outcome, not the tool. People pay to stop losing what they know.

**BUILD:** Write one sentence offer: I build a private second brain that answers your
own knowledge, for X. Fill in the X.

**PROOF:** "I sold three second brain setups in a week at a price I would never charge
for a document."

### Lesson 5.3 — The pitch that sells a second brain

**Sell the pain of forgetting, then show the brain that ends it.**

The pitch in four beats:
- Name the pain: you write things down and never find them again
- Agitate: every lost note is a lost decision, a repeated mistake, a client you had to
  ask twice
- Show the brain: open their notes as a living graph and let JARVIS answer a real
  question in front of them
- Close: this is your knowledge, working for you, private, forever

Do the demo live. The moment their own notes light up and answer them, the sale is
mostly done.

**BUILD:** Record a two minute demo on your own brain. Post it. That video is your best
salesperson.

**PROOF:** "I stopped explaining and just did the demo on their notes. They said take my
money before I finished."
